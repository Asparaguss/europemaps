Privacy, explained.

What stays on your watch, what a map download sends, and how to contact us about your data.

Privacy policy · Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version

Your live map position is processed on your watch. A download sends the location you choose, or a snapshot of your position for “Download around me.” The service keeps installation credentials and limited security and quota records. EuropeMaps does not add advertising. Optional service statistics can count map downloads and watch models. If you buy Premium, purchase matching and email verification connect your entitlement to one watch installation. These processes are described below.

2. What stays on your watch

The app uses the watch’s location and available heading information to show your position, follow movement and orient the map while those features are in use. It does not continuously upload your GPS position or a walking track to our server.

Downloaded maps, the active-area selection, display preferences, download settings and automatically generated access credentials are stored on the watch. The app generates a random installation identifier; it is not your Garmin account name, email address or watch serial number.

You can use saved maps without contacting the map server. The app does not request your Garmin password or read your existing Garmin activity history. If you choose Activity recording, it creates a native activity file on the watch, including the recorded position track and activity measurements. Saving makes that activity available for normal synchronization with Garmin Connect under Garmin’s own privacy terms; the map server does not receive that recorded track.

3. What a map request sends

  • Location search: the place name, street/path name or coordinates entered in the download field are sent to find matching locations.
  • Area preparation: the chosen centre coordinates, area size and short area name are sent to prepare the requested map. “Download around me” sends a snapshot of your current coordinates when you use that option. Prepared area centres are aligned to a map grid.
  • Automatic registration: a random installation identifier and access secret are sent over HTTPS so the service can authorize downloads. The service stores keyed hashes of these values, registration and expiry dates, and revocation status.
  • Website map selection and GPX imports: a temporary watch code links a browser selection to that installation. When you choose a map centre or import a GPX route, the website sends the selected coordinates, route name and route points needed to prepare the transfer. The original GPX file is parsed in your browser; the service receives route data rather than the original file. Treat route coordinates and names as potentially private. Prepared corridor geometry and coverage can remain in the shared map cache.
  • Optional service statistics: when enabled, the service records per-area download starts and map-response/payload totals, separately from watch identities. Starts can include retries and do not prove a completed download. Route-corridor statistics can include a nearby town/region and an approximate 5 km grid location. This coarsening does not guarantee anonymity. A separate model collector can associate the reported watch model with a pseudonymous installation record; this is a hardware model, not a watch serial number. The active-installation overview counts quota-accounted requests, not users connected right now.
  • Security and quotas: the service processes the connection’s IP address and keeps short-lived IP-derived hashes for rate limits, together with per-installation request counts, download-byte counters and area-request counts. These are operational controls, rather than optional visitor analytics.
  • Technical records: limited connection/error information may be processed by the hosting infrastructure for reliability and security. The supplied server configuration disables ordinary HTTP access logging and filters request URLs and authentication headers from its proxy error logs.

Prepared maps and their area names, centres and sizes may be reused through a shared cache. Other authorized installations requesting that area can receive the same prepared map metadata. Use public place names, not personal notes, as area names. The cache is not a record of your continuous movements.

If you contact us directly, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. If you use the optional contact form on our Buy Me a Coffee contact page, Buy Me a Coffee collects the submission and forwards it to our inbox; messages may include your email address, name and text. Its privacy notice applies to its handling of the form. Please do not send passwords or access secrets.

3a. Premium purchases, activation and restoration

This section applies when Premium is offered and enabled. Basic map use does not require you to give EuropeMaps an email address. Premium unlocks larger map areas and additional saved routes for one active watch installation; it does not remove download quotas or technical storage limits.

Purchase information

Premium purchases take place through Buy Me a Coffee, which hosts the shop and processes the payment. We receive signed purchase and refund notifications that may contain the supporter email, name or message, transaction identifier, purchased item, amount, currency and payment status. Our licensing code checks the signature, configured product, successful payment status, and positive EUR purchase amount. The server does not store the full notification, supporter name, or message in the license database. We do not receive full card numbers or card security codes. Buy Me a Coffee retains its own order and payment records under its privacy notice. A verified refund notification marks the transaction refunded; later retries of the original purchase event cannot restore it.

The license database retains the transaction identifier, a keyed digest of the normalized purchase email and whether the payment was refunded. The digest allows later purchase matching without keeping the raw purchase email in that database. It is pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous data. The active license binding records that digest, the current installation identifier and a change timestamp. Purchase and license records are separate from optional map-download and model statistics and are not added to those reports.

Email verification and replacement watches

When you request activation or restoration, you enter the email used for the purchase and a temporary code from the current watch. The watch code links the request to authenticated installation credentials; it is not proof of a physical watch serial number or Garmin account ownership. For a matching unrefunded purchase, we send a short-lived verification code from europemaps@etik.com. We use Infomaniak to deliver it. Infomaniak receives the recipient address, verification message and delivery information; map coordinates and GPX routes are not included in this email.

The activation service temporarily stores the recipient address and email code in its delivery queue. Verification challenges separately contain the email digest, intended installation, a keyed code proof, expiry and attempt count. Successful verification activates that installation and replaces any previous server-side watch binding. You do not need the old watch or a new payment. No separate EuropeMaps password is created. We rate-limit requests and verification attempts and use keyed client/address-derived counters to prevent abuse. Verification messages contain no marketing or tracking pixels added by EuropeMaps.

Manual recovery when you lose email access

You can contact asparaguss@proton.me from a replacement email address to request a manual ownership review. We use the correspondence, transaction reference and relevant purchase evidence to assess the request, including checking Buy Me a Coffee purchase records or asking for verification through the payment provider. A reference or email address alone is not sufficient. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment details, identity documents or GPX files. Support correspondence is handled in the support mailbox and is not copied into the license database.

After approved recovery, the database links the original purchase email digest to a replacement email digest, preserving original transaction matching for retries and refunds. A recovery history records the transaction identifier, previous and replacement keyed email digests and change timestamp. These remain pseudonymous personal data. We revoke the previous online watch binding and outstanding verification challenges for the recovered license. You must verify the replacement mailbox normally before activating a watch. Recovery history and support correspondence are separate from optional map-download statistics. Recovery mappings and audit records have no automatic purge; retention of support correspondence, operational records and backups requires operator review according to the purposes and obligations described below.

The watch stores a small server/installation-scoped entitlement cache, including its status and last refresh time. Paid status may remain usable offline for up to 30 days. Server-side checks still apply to new large-map downloads. An old watch that stays offline cannot be revoked immediately. Previously downloaded maps and routes are not remotely deleted by a transfer or refund.

4. Why we process this information

We process map requests to provide the service you ask for, and installation/security information to authorize access, allocate shared resources and prevent abuse. Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are performance of the requested service under Article 6(1)(b) and our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service under Article 6(1)(f).

Where consent is required for access to device location, that access depends on your location permissions and use of the location-based features (Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw that consent by changing the relevant permissions or stopping use of those features. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

For Premium, purchase matching, verification, restoration and refund handling are necessary to provide the paid service you request (Article 6(1)(b)). Our legitimate interests in protecting paid entitlements and preventing misuse support the security controls and necessary refund/replay records (Article 6(1)(f)). Where a particular accounting or other record is legally required, Article 6(1)(c) may apply to that record. You can contact us about the basis or retention of your specific record. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising profiles. Optional service statistics help us understand download demand, service capacity and supported watch models. If the details needed for a download are not provided, the service cannot prepare that area. Previously saved maps remain usable.

5. How long information is kept

Retention depends on the type of record and its operational purpose:

  • On-watch data: saved maps and preferences remain until you delete or replace them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Installation records: credentials normally have a 90-day validity period that can be extended on renewal. Expiry does not automatically erase the registration record. Records can remain for renewal, access control and abuse prevention, or until removed in response to a verified request where no continuing reason requires retention.
  • Quota counters: older per-installation daily/monthly counters are pruned during later authenticated requests. Short-lived IP-based limits are similarly pruned when the relevant requests occur. They are not deleted by a guaranteed background deadline; idle services can retain old entries longer. Aggregate historical service totals do not identify an installation.
  • Area requests and shared maps: request history is capacity-limited and completed entries are replaced as the queue fills. Shared prepared maps are evicted as the cache fills. There is no fixed time-based expiry for every request row or cached area.
  • Pairing and route preparation: codes expire after ten minutes and are for one transfer. Expired pairing rows are removed when pairing cleanup runs; this is not a guaranteed background deletion deadline. Route-preparation inputs are removed as requests are processed or cleaned up. Prepared corridor coverage remains subject to the shared-cache rules above.
  • Optional statistics: daily download/model counters use a rolling 31-UTC-calendar-day window and are pruned when collection runs. Statistical corridor-location metadata is removed when its area has no retained daily counters. Latest model observations are removed when their installation has not been seen within the retained window. Cleanup runs during collection, so an idle service can retain old entries longer; active installations refresh their last-seen model observation.
  • Premium purchases and bindings: the licensing code has no automatic time-based deletion of transaction identifiers, email digests, refund state or active bindings. We keep the records needed to maintain and restore the entitlement and prevent replay of refunded purchases. We handle verified deletion requests and review continued need in light of the license, refund/dispute status and any applicable legal retention requirement; deleting records needed for restoration can prevent later automatic recovery. Refund records are not automatically erased when access ends. Copies in operator backups are separate and are not automatically erased by a database change.
  • Verification and email queues: email codes and challenges expire after ten minutes; expiry is not a promise that every database row is physically erased at that instant. Successful activation removes challenges for that purchase email and their queued messages. Expired challenges are removed when another verification request runs. While the mail worker is running, expired/attempt-exhausted mail is removed by its cleanup loop; successfully submitted messages are removed from the local queue. If the worker is stopped, queue data may remain until it resumes or another verification request runs. Provider queues, delivery logs, recipient inboxes and backups follow their own retention rules. Verification rate counters are pruned when later verification activity runs, not by a guaranteed background deadline.
  • Entitlement cache: the watch checks the paid offline cache for up to 30 days after refresh. That validity limit does not erase the stored cache at the deadline; it is overwritten by a later entitlement update or removed with app storage.
  • Support messages: retained for handling the request and necessary follow-up; deletion may be requested using the contact above, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Security or legal obligations may require limited information to be retained longer. Deleting a map or uninstalling the watch app does not automatically delete server-side records.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can remove saved maps using MENU → Saved maps and linked routes/corridors through MENU → Route Navigation → select route → Delete route + map, stop using location features, change device permissions where available, or uninstall the app.

Subject to the law that applies to you, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent where it is the legal basis. You may also complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, including in the country where you live or work or where you believe an infringement occurred.

For a request, email asparaguss@proton.me with “EuropeMaps privacy request” in the subject. For ordinary free use, installations are not linked to an email account, so your email alone may not identify the record. For Premium, the verified purchase email can locate associated licensing records. We still need to verify ownership before disclosing or changing them. A deletion request is not a refund request; contact us separately about a purchase problem, and note that deleting licensing records may affect automatic restoration. We may ask for reasonable, non-secret information to locate and verify the relevant record. Please do not send passwords or authentication secrets. Where the GDPR applies, requests are normally answered within one month, with any permitted extension explained.
